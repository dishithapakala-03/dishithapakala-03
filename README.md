## 💡 Featured Projects (Business Analyst Portfolio)

### 🚗 Patent 1 — Real-Time Pothole Detection & Alert System
**Domain:** Intelligent Transportation Systems | Cloud Computing | IoT

A cloud-enabled AI system that detects potholes in real time using **YOLOv4** computer vision and **ESP32 camera modules**, transmitting GPS coordinates to **Google Firebase** and alerting drivers via mobile app. Involves integration of STM32 microcontroller, ultrasonic sensors, and adaptive headlight control.

**BA Lens:** System requirements definition, stakeholder mapping (drivers, municipal authorities, road crews), IoT data flow architecture, real-time alert notification design.

> 📋 Patent No: 202541021974 | Filed: March 2025 | Published: March 2025

### 🚂 Patent 2 — Automatic Monitoring System for Railway Accident Prevention
**Domain:** Real-Time Safety Systems | IoT | Embedded Systems

**BA Lens:** Safety-critical requirements documentation, risk register development, stakeholder communication across railway operations teams.

> 📋 Patent No: 202341085234 | Filed: December 2023 | Published: January 2024

### 🌿 Research — Plant Disease Detection Using Deep Learning
**Domain:** Precision Agriculture | Computer Vision | Transfer Learning

Developed an **EfficientNetB0**-based multi-class classification model achieving **98.35% accuracy** across 7 disease categories (apple, cherry, blueberry) using 13,096 training images. Leveraged transfer learning, data augmentation, and two-phase fine-tuning.

**BA Lens:** Defining performance KPIs (precision, recall, F1), translating model outputs into agricultural business recommendations, dataset requirements analysis.

> 📖 Co-authored research paper | VIT-AP University, 2024

### 👥 IBM Employee Attrition & Performance Analysis
**Domain:** HR Analytics | Predictive Modeling | Business Intelligence

Analyzed IBM employee data to identify attrition risk factors using **Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, and SVM**. Applied feature engineering, correlation analysis, chi-square testing, and Power BI visualization to deliver actionable workforce retention insights.

**BA Lens:** Stakeholder problem definition (HR + Operations), data requirements gathering, business insight reporting, retention strategy recommendations derived from predictive outputs.

> 🏢 Internship Project — INTRAIN TECH, Bangalore | Oct–Nov 2023
